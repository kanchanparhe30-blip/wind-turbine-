# wind-turbine-
Wind Turbine Project

1. Introduction

A wind turbine is a device that converts wind energy into electrical energy. Our project aims to generate electricity using wind power.

2. Problem Statement

Electricity consumption is increasing day by day. We need renewable and eco-friendly sources of energy.

3. Objectives

- To generate electricity using wind energy.
- To understand the working of a wind turbine.
- To use renewable energy.
- To reduce dependence on conventional energy sources.

4. Working Principle

Wind rotates the turbine blades. The rotating blades drive a generator, which converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.

5. Components Used

- Turbine Blades
- DC Motor / Generator
- Supporting Structure
- Connecting Wires
- LED

6. Fabrication and Assembly

The turbine structure and blades are prepared and assembled with the generator and other components.

7. Testing

The prototype is tested by providing airflow to rotate the blades and checking electricity generation.

8. Results

The project demonstrates the conversion of wind energy into electrical energy.

9. Conclusion

This project helps us understand renewable energy and electricity generation using wind power.
